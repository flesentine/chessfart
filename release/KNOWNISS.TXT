CHESS FART 1.0.0 - KNOWN ISSUES / PLATFORM NOTES
================================================

RELEASE BLOCKERS
No known host-test, rules-engine, save/load, CPU, packaging, browser, or
DOSBox-smoke P0/P1 blockers at the time 1.0.0 is cut. See
`docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` in the source repository for the verification
matrix.

PLATFORM NOTES
- The reference target is 16-bit DOS built with Open Watcom.
- Sound Blaster playback uses simple polling/direct-DAC output rather than DMA.
  This is intentionally small but timing can vary on unusual hardware/emulators.
- CPU difficulty is bounded by node/time budgets, so apparent think time depends
  on emulated/real CPU speed.
- Mouse support requires a Microsoft-compatible DOS mouse driver. Keyboard play
  is complete and does not require a mouse.
- CI exercises a DOSBox platform smoke program; full interactive sessions on
  physical 386/486 systems, MS-DOS/FreeDOS variants, and third-party mouse
  drivers remain manual/community validation targets.
- Real Sound Blaster 1.x/2.0/Pro/16 combinations remain unverified hardware
  targets; AUTO falls back to PC speaker when Sound Blaster init fails.
- No music is included in 1.0.0; all audio is SFX plus PC-speaker fallback.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
The versioned CHESSFRT.SAV and CHESSFRT.CFG formats remain those introduced in
Build 9. The 1.0 release does not change game-state serialization from RC1.
