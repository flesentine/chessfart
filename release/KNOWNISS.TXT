CHESS FART 1.0.0-rc1 - KNOWN ISSUES / PLATFORM NOTES
=====================================================

RELEASE BLOCKERS
No known host-test, rules-engine, save/load, CPU, packaging, or DOSBox smoke
blockers at the time RC1 is cut. See docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md in the source
repository for the verification matrix.

PLATFORM NOTES
- The reference target is 16-bit DOS built with Open Watcom.
- Sound Blaster playback uses simple polling/direct-DAC output rather than DMA.
  This is intentionally small but timing can vary on unusual hardware/emulators.
- CPU difficulty is bounded by node/time budgets, so apparent think time depends
  on emulated/real CPU speed.
- Mouse support requires a Microsoft-compatible DOS mouse driver. Keyboard play
  is complete and does not require a mouse.
- RC1 CI exercises a DOSBox platform smoke program; full interactive sessions
  on a range of physical 386/486 machines remain community/manual validation.
- No music is included in RC1; all audio is SFX plus PC-speaker fallback.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
The versioned CHESSFRT.SAV and CHESSFRT.CFG formats remain those introduced in
Build 9. Build 12 does not change game-state serialization.
